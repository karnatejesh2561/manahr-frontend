## ✨ Pro Tips for Success

1. **Quality > Quantity**
   - 5 great blog posts > 20 mediocre ones
   - 5 quality backlinks > 50 spammy ones

2. **Consistency Matters**
   - Publish 2-3 blog posts monthly
   - Update Google Search Console monthly
   - Monitor analytics weekly

3. **User First**
   - Write for humans, not robots
   - Fast loading is SEO
   - Mobile optimization is critical

4. **Build Relationships**
   - Engage on Twitter, LinkedIn, GitHub
   - Guest post on established blogs
   - Collaborate with other developers

5. **Stay Updated**
   - Follow Google Search Central
   - Subscribe to SEO newsletters
   - Test new strategies monthly

## ❓ FAQ

**Q: When will we rank for "ManaTech"?**
A: 3-6 months to see ranking, 6-12 months to be in top 10.

**Q: How many backlinks do we need?**
A: Quality > quantity. 30-50 quality backlinks is better than 200 spammy ones.

**Q: Should we focus on backlinks or content?**
A: Both. Content brings organic traffic, backlinks improve authority.

**Q: What's the most important SEO factor?**
A: User experience. Fast, mobile-friendly, easy to navigate.

**Q: How do we know if it's working?**
A: Check Google Search Console monthly for impressions and rankings.
